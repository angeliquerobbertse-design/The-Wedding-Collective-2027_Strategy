    IVWhere is the opportunity?White space · The regional advantage
    Where the market is open

    The gap is not a better directory.
    It is a different operator.

    Based on the propositions reviewed, these are the areas where we did not find a strong incumbent. Each needs validating in the pilot.

    Regional depth tied to a news audience

    Most platforms reviewed are national directories organised by province. The regional specialists we found are founder-led and cover one region. None is attached to a regional news and lifestyle audience.

    Reaching couples before they search

    Directories serve couples who arrive ready to search. A publisher reaches people earlier, at the dream stage, through general news, lifestyle and social audiences that are not yet on a wedding site.

    Print, digital and social in one sale

    Wedding magazines have largely moved online. Wedding Collection went fully digital in 2016. Outside annual regional guides, few players we found sell regional print, digital and social together.

    A clear ladder for small suppliers

    Free and basic listings are common. What we did not find is a simple path from a R3,500 listing to an integrated regional campaign across several media channels.

    Existing wedding platforms

    Wedding-specific expertise and discovery.

    Volt

    Established publishing brands, regional audiences, editorial capability, print, digital, social and commercial sales infrastructure.

    The opening is a regional, publisher-led wedding ecosystem. It sits alongside the directories rather than competing to be another one.

    The regional advantage

    Weddings are planned
    locally.

    A couple marrying in Cape Town needs different businesses from a couple marrying in Durban or Johannesburg. National platforms handle this with filters. A regional edition is built for it.

    Cape Town

    Local venues, local caterers, local florists, local audience.

    Durban

    A different supplier base and a different editorial agenda.

    Johannesburg

    A different market again, with its own brands and budgets.

    VWhy can Volt play here?The publisher advantage · One title per region
    Why the publisher has an advantage

    Why do this through a publisher,
    not a standalone website?

    Audience

    Existing publishing audiences across IOL and the National Media Group titles. People are already there before they start searching for a wedding.

    Regional footprint

    Regional titles and newsrooms that can operate locally. In the Western Cape pilot that means the Cape Times as the anchor title, IOL for national reach and Cape Community Media titles for local suppliers.

    Content

    Editorial capability and trusted publishing environments. This is what gives a directory authority.

    Distribution

    Digital, social and print. One audience can be reached across several channels and sold as one package.

    Sales

    Existing direct commercial relationships and sales pods. We do not need a new sales force to launch.

    Scale

    One model, adapted to several regions. National lifestyle titles in the portfolio, such as Glamour and House & Garden, give a route to multi-region brand campaigns.

    One title per region · chosen for audience fit

    Each edition has one anchor title, chosen on who reads it rather than on how many. We looked for people at the life stage of getting married, a strong share of women, spending power, and an environment that suits a premium wedding brand.     Strategy recommendations · for discussion
    Take entries where the audience is

    Cape Times has 872 Instagram followers and 97,838 on Facebook. Daily News has 4,340 and 190,834.18 Take entries on Facebook as well, and boost the Instagram vote.

    Borrow Glamour's Instagram

    Glamour readers are 84% women and 36% aged 25–34.19 A joint post puts the vote in front of the right audience.

    Capture who they are

    A comment gives us no way to reach the person again. Add a "Join the 2028 Brides list" sign-up with a budget question and a magazine pre-order.

    Let partners fund the prize

    Sell the prize to a venue, a photographer and a jeweller as Partnership placements.

    Rules and consent

    Publish competition terms, and get POPIA consent to feature stories and keep entrants' details.

    What we measure

    Entries, story reads, votes, follower growth, sign-ups, and magazine sales to the list.

    Risks · what needs to be solved

    What could
    go wrong.

    Strategy's view of the execution challenge, and how the pilot is designed to deal with it.

    ChallengeWhy it mattersHow we address it
    Building the initial supplier base

    An empty directory gives couples no reason to visit.

    Seed with editorial features and a founding-supplier offer, focused on priority categories.

    Regional density

    Thin coverage in any category weakens discovery.

    Start in a tight geography (Cape Town and the Winelands) before covering the whole province.

    Maintaining quality

    Low-quality listings damage the premium position.

    Set basic listing criteria, with editorial curation for anything featured.

    Becoming "just another directory"

    Without editorial, we lose the distinction.

    Editorial leads every edition. The directory is one layer of the product, not the product.

    Sales adoption

    A complex product will not get sold.

    Five simple packages, a sales kit, and incentives aligned with the pilot.

    Editorial and commercial alignment

    Blurred lines erode trust.

    Labelling rules and editor sign-off, agreed before launch.

    Development requirements

    A full directory build could delay the pilot.

    Scope a minimum directory for the pilot and confirm the build route with Product and Tech.

    Supplier retention

    One-off buyers do not build a platform.

    Report performance each season and offer renewals around the wedding calendar.

    A premium consumer experience

    Ad clutter would undercut the brand.

    Set ad-density limits and design standards for the wedding environment.
